Found us on Google Maps? The cats aren't at the pin.

We're Pune's virtual adoption centre for indie cats. Every cat lives with the person who rescued it, one message away.

Before you book a rickshaw: please don't travel to the map location. There are no cats there, and no office. Everything happens online, and this page shows you how.

Why is there nothing to visit?

Because our cats don't live in one place. Every cat is with the person who rescued it — real homes all across Pune and PCMC: Baner, Kothrud, Viman Nagar, Kharadi, Pimple Saudagar and beyond. There's no shelter building, no cages, no counter.

The map pin is there so people can find us online. This website and Instagram are all there is.

So how do I meet a cat?

1. Open the cats looking for homes — photos, age, neighbourhood and the rescuer’s contact, all on one page. Everything posts on Instagram too.

2. Found one you like? The rescuer's contact is in the post. Message them directly — they know the cat best.

3. They'll ask about your home and family. There is a form, and it says why they ask. Then you meet the cat at their place and, if it's a match, take your new family member home. Free — a cat is not a commodity.

The full process, in simple words, is here: How adoption works.

Can I call someone instead?

We don't have a phone number — we're a group of volunteers with day jobs, and everything here runs on messages. DM us on Instagram. Replies can be slow; if it's been a while, message again.

Found a cat that needs help?

If you've rescued a healthy cat or kittens that need a home, tell us here: Send us a cat. If an animal is injured or in danger, the Emergency page has what we know.
