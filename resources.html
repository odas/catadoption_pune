Emergency & NGOs

If a cat is hurt or in danger right now, the NGO numbers are below. If you've found a kitten on its own, read the first box before you call.

⚠️ Is it an emergency?

It is if the cat is hurt, bleeding or can't stand, or in danger right now: a busy road, an open drain, dogs, ants already on it, heavy rain with nowhere dry, or a kitten that is cold and floppy when you pick it up. Then it needs help today, so call the NGOs below.

A kitten on its own usually isn't one. Its mother is almost certainly nearby (she leaves the litter to eat, and she won't come back while someone is standing over it). So watch from a distance for an hour or two before you pick it up, and ask the watchman whether somebody already feeds it.

📞 NGO directory — Pune / PCMC

Their lines are busy. If nobody picks up, keep going down the list, and send a report in writing too (next box).

  • Saahas for Animals — 84595 92034, call or WhatsApp. Their rescue helpline runs 24/7.
  • Animal Rescue Trust — WhatsApp or SMS 72629 55444. No calls on this number.
  • Karma Foundation — 83909 44337
  • Mission Possible — 99218 81894
  • SWAP, which used to be SPCA Pune — 020‑2644 4960. A vet hospital in Bhavani Peth.
  • Blue Cross — 1800‑419‑9005, toll-free. A clinic you take the cat to. They don't do rescues.
  • RESQ — 98909 99111, 11 am to 5 pm. Only for a cat stuck somewhere, like a height, a drain or a tight space. Otherwise they take wildlife cases only.

Each number is the one the NGO publishes itself, checked against their own websites on 26 September 2026.

We're volunteers, not an NGO, and we can't come out to a cat. Pune has only a few rescue NGOs for a very big city, and they're set up for animals that are hurt. A report with a clear photo, the exact spot and what's wrong is what a volunteer can act on.
