Rescuers — sending us a cat? Send it like this.
  1. Clear daytime photos of the cat or kitten.
  2. Age. Your best guess is fine — we post kittens from about 1½ months old.
  3. Location and contact details.
  4. Dewormed? Vet check done? Litter or house-trained?
  5. Over 2 months — vaccinated?
  6. Can you keep them until they're adopted? And may we tag you?

Then give it 2–5 days. AI works instantly, but we need some time to hand-check & approve the DM

Write in whatever language is comfortable — English, Hinglish, Marathi are all fine. Write it like you'd tell a friend.

Want to meet one of them?

Contact the rescuer on the card — they know the cat best, and we don't stand in between. They'll ask about your home and family; that's not a test to fail, it's how the match is made. They'll also ask whether your windows and balconies are netted, so it's worth reading that first.

One quiet ask: if you're open to a grown cat rather than a kitten, say so. Kittens go quickly. Adult cats wait months for the same message, and they are very often the easier ones to live with.
