How an indie cat finds a home here.

Pune has no shelter for a healthy cat, not even a government one. So every cat on our board is living with the person who rescued it.

1 · Browse

See the cats looking for homes, with photos, age, area and the rescuer's note. New cats post on Instagram too.

2 · Connect

The rescuer's number is on every post. They know the cat best, and they'll ask about your home. That's how they find the right match.

3 · Adopt

Meet the cat, collect its vaccination records, and go home together. You don't pay anything, and neither does the rescuer.
