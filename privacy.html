Privacy

Short, because there is little to say: this site collects almost nothing.

What we collect

Nothing you type. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no comment boxes and no forms on this site. The adopter form is text you copy and send to a rescuer yourself; it never passes through us.

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookie and stores no personal data. We see how many people opened a page and which link brought them, never who.

Cookies

This site sets none, so there is no cookie banner. Links to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and the shops below take you to sites with their own policies.

Affiliate links

Some articles carry affiliate links: things we use ourselves, linked through Cuelinks. Clicking one sends you through a redirect that tells the shop we referred you; if you buy, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. The shop, not this site, may set a cookie for that. Every page with such links says so above the first one, and there are none in adoption pages or in care basics.

Contact details on the board

Rescuers' phone numbers and Instagram handles appear on the adoption board because the rescuer asked us to list them, in the DM that submitted the cat. A rescuer who wants a listing changed or removed can DM us on Instagram.
