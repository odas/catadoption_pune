# For a Claude Code session opened on this repo

This is the live site: a push to `main` deploys to catadoption.in within minutes. The operating
records, the reasons behind the rules and the affiliate plan are kept privately and are not in
this repository, so a session here is working with part of the picture. Assume a rule you cannot
see the reason for has one.

- **Work on a branch; do not push `main`.** The owner publishes from her laptop after reading the
  diff. If she asks for a direct push herself (a time-limited one-liner has happened), say exactly
  what will deploy and push only that.
- **The rules in `README.md` are decisions, not defaults.** `learn.html` never carries affiliate
  links; `adoptable.html` is generated and never hand-edited; the palette and fonts are locked.
- **Affiliate links are made by hand in the network's panel and pasted in**, one deliberate link at
  a time, with `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`. The site loads no link-conversion script; do not
  add one. That keeps the education pages clean by construction and every earning link a choice.
- **Leave a record.** Say what you did and why in the commit message, and if the session produced
  findings the owner should keep, write them as a note she can download and file privately.
