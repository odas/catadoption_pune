Cat care

The essentials first — kept short, because you'll learn most of it from the cat. Then the specific problems, answered.

Start here: the basics

🪟 Netting your home first

Before you bring a cat home, net your windows and balconies. Cats are curious and fast. Netting (not grilling) is safe and effective — vendors in most Pune neighbourhoods can install it in a day.

🍽️ What to feed an indie cat

Indie cats thrive on a mix of good-quality dry kibble and wet food. Avoid milk — most adult cats are lactose intolerant. Fresh water, always. Your rescuer will tell you what the cat is already used to. Just rescued a kitten, or feeding a pregnant stray? What to feed a rescued kitten in Pune is the longer answer.

🧹 The litter box

One box per cat, in a quiet corner the cat can reach at night, scooped every day. Cats arrive knowing how to use it; when something goes wrong it is usually the box, the spot or the litter, not the cat. Where the box goes when the rains come, and which litter survives the humidity: the litter box in a Pune monsoon.

🩺 First vet visit & vaccines

Schedule a vet visit within the first two weeks. Bring any vaccination records from the rescuer. Core vaccines, deworming, and a health check are the basics. Ask about spay/neuter if not already done.

🐾 The indie cat myth

Indies aren't less loving because they're not breeds. Many are deeply affectionate. What varies is personality — your rescuer will tell you if a cat is a lap cat, an observer, or somewhere in between.

🏠 Settling in — the first week

Give a new cat one quiet room first. Let them come to you. Hissing on day one is normal. Most cats decompress over 3–7 days and reveal their real personality within a month. The hair on everything starts the same week — what actually works against it. The first accident usually does too; what cleans it, and what in the cupboard to keep off the cat. What to have ready before the cat arrives is the day-one list.

⚖️ Cat feeders' legal rights

If you feed community cats and face harassment, you have legal protection. The PCA Act and recent court rulings recognise feeding rights. The steps for street feeders are written for dogs, and the protections are the same. Full cat-feeder guide coming soon; for a lawyer who takes animal cases, see Emergency.
