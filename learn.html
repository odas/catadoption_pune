Cat care

The essentials first — kept short, because you'll learn most of it from the cat. Then the specific problems, answered.

Start here: the basics

🪟 Netting your home first

Before you bring a cat home, net your windows and balconies. Cats are curious and fast. Netting (not grilling) is safe and effective — vendors in most Pune neighbourhoods can install it in a day. Where it goes, what it costs and why a grill is not enough: netting a Pune flat for a cat.

🍽️ What to feed an indie cat

Cats need meat; that part is settled. Beyond it, even long-time rescuers disagree, so check with a vet you trust. Avoid milk — most adult cats are lactose intolerant, and roti and milk cannot be the main meal: can cats live on doodh-roti? is the gentle version of why. Fresh water, always. Your rescuer will tell you what the cat is already used to. Just rescued a kitten, or feeding a pregnant stray? What to feed a rescued kitten in Pune is the longer answer. Found one outside and not sure whether to pick it up at all? Start with I found a kitten, what do I do.

🧹 The litter box

One box per cat, in a quiet corner the cat can reach at night, scooped every day. Cats arrive knowing how to use it; when something goes wrong it is usually the box, the spot or the litter, not the cat. Where the box goes when the rains come, and which litter survives the humidity: the litter box in a Pune monsoon.

🩺 First vet visit & vaccines

Schedule a vet visit within the first two weeks. Bring any vaccination records from the rescuer. Core vaccines, deworming, and a health check are the basics. Ask about spay/neuter if not already done.

🐾 The indie cat myth

Indies aren't less loving because they're not breeds. Many are deeply affectionate. What varies is personality — your rescuer will tell you if a cat is a lap cat, an observer, or somewhere in between.

🏠 Settling in — the first week

Give a new cat one quiet room first. Let them come to you. Hissing on day one is normal, and so is a cat that spends its first day under the bed. Most of the indies we place settle in days rather than weeks: kittens fastest, and an adult that let itself be caught is usually halfway there already. You will find the 3-3-3 rule online — three days, three weeks, three months — but it describes shelter cats abroad, mostly surrendered or trapped, and it runs slower than what we see here. Either way the real rule is that every cat is different, and one that takes its time has not gone wrong. The hair on everything starts the same week — what actually works against it. The first accident usually does too; what cleans it, and what in the cupboard to keep off the cat. What to have ready before the cat arrives is the day-one list.

⚖️ Cat feeders' legal rights

If you feed community cats and face harassment, you have legal protection. The PCA Act and recent court rulings recognise feeding rights. The steps for street feeders are written for dogs, and the protections are the same. Full cat-feeder guide coming soon; for a lawyer who takes animal cases, see Emergency.
